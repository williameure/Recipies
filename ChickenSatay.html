    2. 2
      Season the chicken with salt. Whisk together 1 tbsp neutral oil, lime juice, curry powder, brown sugar, and granulated garlic. Toss with the chicken, cover, and marinate at room temperature for at least 20 minutes.
    3. 3
      Preheat the oven to 400°F. Toss the cooked Coconut Rice with 2 tbsp neutral oil, spread onto an unlined baking sheet, and bake for 15–18 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
    4. 4
      Thread the chicken onto skewers. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large oven-proof skillet over high heat. Add the skewers and cook, undisturbed, until lightly charred. Flip and cook 3–4 more minutes. Transfer to the oven to finish cooking, about 5 minutes.
    5. 5
      Whisk the Serrano Garlic Sauce and olive oil together until smooth. Add the lettuce, cabbage, cucumbers, carrots, peanuts, scallions, and serranos. Toss to combine.
    6. 6
      Right before serving, fold in the crispy coconut rice. Divide between two bowls, top with the chicken skewers, and garnish with cilantro and extra peanuts.
    Notes

    Fold in the crispy rice at the very last moment — it loses its crunch quickly once tossed.
