Crispy Sesame Chicken Bowls

with coconut rice & serrano slaw

—   —   —
Serves
2
people
Time 45 min
Ingredients
Chicken
    Slaw
      Instructions
      1. 1
        Prep

        Prepare the Spicy Soy Marinade and Coconut Rice using the recipes below.

      2. 2
        Marinate

        Cover the chicken with parchment and pound to ½-inch thick. Marinate in ¼ cup of the Spicy Soy Marinade for 20 minutes.

      3. 3
        Bread

        Set up three shallow bowls: (1) flour + pinch salt, (2) whisked egg, (3) panko + sesame seeds + pinch salt. Dredge the chicken in flour, dip in egg, then press firmly into the breadcrumb mixture.

      4. 4
        Fry

        Heat a ¼-inch layer of neutral oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Cook the chicken until deeply golden and crisp, 3–4 minutes per side. When internal temperature reaches 165°F, transfer to a paper towel-lined plate, season with salt, and rest 3–4 minutes.

      5. 5
        Slaw

        Whisk mayonnaise, lime juice, brown sugar, and salt until smooth. Add cabbage, scallions, serranos, and cucumber. Toss to coat.

      6. 6
        Serve

        Divide Coconut Rice between two bowls. Top each with the crispy chicken and slaw. Garnish with cilantro and eggs if using.

      Resting is keyThe 3–4 minute rest after frying keeps the crust crispy instead of steaming.
