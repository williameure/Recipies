    2. 2
      Cover the chicken with parchment and pound to ½-inch thick. Marinate in ¼ cup of the Spicy Soy Marinade for 20 minutes.
    3. 3
      Set up three shallow bowls: (1) flour + pinch salt, (2) whisked egg, (3) panko + sesame seeds + pinch salt.
    4. 4
      Dredge the marinated chicken in flour, dip in egg, then press firmly into the breadcrumb mixture.
    5. 5
      Heat a ¼-inch layer of neutral oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Cook the chicken until deeply golden and crisp, 3–4 minutes per side. When internal temperature reaches 165°F, transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and season with salt. Rest 3–4 minutes.
    6. 6
      Whisk mayonnaise, lime juice, brown sugar, and salt until smooth. Add cabbage, scallions, serranos, and cucumber. Toss to coat.
    7. 7
      Divide Coconut Rice between two bowls. Top each with the crispy chicken and slaw. Garnish with cilantro and eggs if using.
    Notes

    The resting period after frying is important — it keeps the crust crispy instead of steaming.

    Spicy Soy Marinade makes ¼ cup
    Ingredients
    • 3 tbspsoy sauce
    • 1½ tbspwarm water
    • 2 tsphoney
    • 1 tspsriracha
    Instructions
    1. 1

    Leftover marinade keeps refrigerated for up to 1 week. Use for the optional marinated eggs below.

    Spicy Soy Marinated Eggs optional — makes 2 eggs
    Ingredients
    • 2large eggs
    • —leftover Spicy Soy Marinade from above
    Instructions
    1. 1
      Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Lower eggs in gently and cook for exactly 8 minutes.
    2. 2
      Transfer immediately to an ice bath. Once cool, peel the eggs in the water for a smooth exterior.
    3. 3
      Pour the leftover marinade over the peeled eggs, cover, and refrigerate. Remove from marinade after 24 hours maximum.

    Coconut Rice makes 2 cups
    Ingredients
    • ¾ cupbasmati or long-grain white rice
    • —¾ of a 13.5 oz can full-fat coconut milk (~10 oz)
    • ½ cupwater
    • 1½ tspgranulated sugar
    • ½ tspkosher salt
    Instructions
    1. 1
      Combine all ingredients in a medium saucepan, bring to a boil over high heat.
    2. 2
      Stir once, reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook for 20–22 minutes.
    3. 3
      Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for 10 minutes before fluffing.

    Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
