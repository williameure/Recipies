    2. 2
      Let the pizza dough rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes, up to 1 hour.
    3. 3
      Preheat the oven to 500°F with a rack in the top position. Place a large cast-iron skillet in the oven to preheat.
    4. 4
      Gently stretch the dough on a lightly floured surface into a circle roughly the size of the skillet.
    5. 5
      Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven. Drizzle in the olive oil, then lay in the dough, letting it rise slightly on the sides. Spread the Peanut Sauce over the dough, leaving a ½-inch border. Top with Chili Crunch Chicken, cheese, and carrots. Bake 10–12 minutes until golden brown.
    6. 6
      Transfer to a cutting board. Top with cilantro, scallions, serranos, and chopped peanuts. Cut into 4 slices and serve immediately.
    Notes

    Cold dough is impossible to stretch — room temperature rest is not optional.

    The preheated cast-iron gives the crust a crispier bottom than a pizza stone.
