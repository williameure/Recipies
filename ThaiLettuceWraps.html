    3. 3

    For a more filling meal, serve with Coconut Rice on the side (recipe below).

    Chili Crunch Chicken makes 2 cups
    Ingredients
    • 1 lbground chicken
    • ½shallot, minced
    • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
    • —½-inch knob ginger, minced
    • ½ bunchscallion whites, thinly sliced
    • 2 tbspoyster sauce
    • 1½ tbspsoy sauce
    • ½ tbsptoasted sesame oil
    • 2 tbspchili crunch
    • —neutral oil, for cooking
    Instructions
    1. 1
      In a medium bowl, combine all ingredients and mix until smooth.
    2. 2
      Heat neutral oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken mixture and cook, undisturbed, for 4–5 minutes until caramelized around the edges. Break up the meat with a spatula, scraping the bottom of the pan, and cook until thoroughly done, another 5–6 minutes.

    Cool completely before storing in an airtight container. Reheat in a hot skillet.

    Serrano Garlic Sauce makes ~⅓ cup

    Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

    Coconut Rice makes ~1 cup — optional
    Ingredients

    • ½ cupfull-fat coconut milk
    • ¼ cupwater
    • ¾ tspgranulated sugar
    • ¼ tspkosher salt
    Instructions
    1. 1

    2. 2

    3. 3
      Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for 10 minutes before fluffing.
