Thai Lettuce Wraps

with chili crunch chicken & two dipping sauces

—   —   —
Serves
2
people
Time 30 min
Ingredients
    Instructions
    1. 1
      Prep

      Prepare the Chili Crunch Chicken, Peanut Sauce, and Serrano Garlic Sauce using the recipes below.

    2. 2
      Heat the chicken

      Heat neutral oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add the Chili Crunch Chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, until hot and slightly caramelized around the edges, about 4 minutes.

    3. 3
      Prep the sauces

      Divide the Serrano Garlic Sauce between two ramekins and garnish with 2–3 slices of serrano. Whisk the Peanut Sauce with warm water until smooth, divide between two ramekins, and garnish with half the chopped peanuts.

    4. 4
      Plate

      Arrange the butter lettuce, carrots, cucumber, cilantro, and scallions on two plates. Arrange the chicken alongside and garnish with the remaining peanuts. Serve with both dipping sauces.

    To eatSpoon the chicken into a lettuce leaf and top with vegetables, peanuts, and sauces.
    OptionalFor a more filling meal, serve with Coconut Rice on the side (recipe below).
