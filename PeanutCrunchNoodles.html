Peanut Crunch Noodles

ramen noodles, peanut sauce & chili crunch chicken

—   —   —
Serves
2
people
Time 30 min
Ingredients
    Instructions
    1. 1
      Prep

      Prepare the Chili Crunch Chicken and Peanut Sauce using the recipes below.

    2. 2
      Aromatics

      Bring a large saucepan of water to a boil. Meanwhile, heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large sauté pan or wok over medium-high heat. Cook the shallot, stirring, until soft and translucent, 3–4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, 15–20 seconds.

    3. 3
      Carrots

      Reduce heat to medium, add the carrots, and cook until tender, about 5 minutes.

    4. 4
      Noodles

      Season the boiling water generously with salt, add the ramen noodles, and cook per package directions. Reserve ⅓ cup of noodle water before draining.

    5. 5
      Toss

      Add the drained noodles to the pan with the shallots and carrots. Pour in the Peanut Sauce and reserved noodle water. Toss vigorously until glossy and emulsified.

    6. 6
      Chicken

      Wipe out the saucepan and return to high heat. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil and the Chili Crunch Chicken. Cook, stirring occasionally, until hot and slightly caramelized, about 4 minutes.

    7. 7
      Serve

      Divide noodles between two bowls and top with the Chili Crunch Chicken. Garnish with serranos, scallions, cilantro, chopped peanuts, and eggs if using.

    Key techniqueToss the noodles and sauce vigorously — the starchy noodle water is the key to a glossy, emulsified sauce.

        Spicy Soy Marinated Eggs

        optional — makes 4 eggs
        Ingredients
          Instructions
          1. 1
            Marinade

            Combine soy sauce, warm water, honey, and sriracha. Stir until honey is fully dissolved.

          2. 2
            Boil

            Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Lower eggs in gently and cook for exactly 8 minutes.

          3. 3
            Ice bath

            Transfer immediately to an ice bath. Once cool, peel the eggs in the water for a smooth exterior.

          4. 4
            Marinate

            Pour ¼ cup of the marinade over the peeled eggs, cover, and refrigerate. Remove from marinade after 24 hours maximum to prevent a rubbery texture.
