      Bring a large saucepan of water to a boil. Meanwhile, heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large sauté pan or wok over medium-high heat. Cook the shallot, stirring, until soft and translucent, 3–4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, 15–20 seconds.
    3. 3

    5. 5
      Add the drained noodles to the pan with shallots and carrots. Pour in the Peanut Sauce and reserved noodle water. Toss vigorously until glossy and emulsified.
    6. 6
      Wipe out the saucepan and return to high heat. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil and the Chili Crunch Chicken. Cook, stirring occasionally, until hot and slightly caramelized, about 4 minutes.
    7. 7
      Divide noodles between two bowls and top with the Chili Crunch Chicken. Garnish with serranos, scallions, cilantro, chopped peanuts, and eggs if using.
    Notes

    Toss the noodles and sauce vigorously — the starchy noodle water is the key to a glossy, emulsified sauce.
